# Tweet drafts

One per asset, under 280 characters, in the game's own voice. Swap `<url>` for wherever the
game is hosted. Attach the file named in brackets. The thread order is a suggestion.

## Launch thread

1. **[v0.8-title-first-night.png]**
   Rug or Not? is a pixel-noir detective game about crypto rug pulls. A folder lands on
   your desk every night. Read the contract, the tokenomics, the chat. Pin what smells.
   Stamp RUG or LEGIT. All cases fictional, all lessons real. Play free in the browser: <url>

2. **[v0.8-clip-solve-a-case.gif]**
   One file, start to finish: open the folder, read through the lens, pin two flags, stamp
   it. Twenty seconds. The report tells you what you missed.

3. **[v0.8-lens-fine-print.png]**
   The magnifier is the whole job. Fine print only shows through it: a fee with no cap, an
   audit for the wrong contract, a lock that expires Friday. Sweep slowly.

4. **[v0.8-case-report.png]**
   Every case ends in a report: what you caught, what got past you, and why it mattered.
   Every red flag you meet gets a page in the notebook. Next time, you'll see it coming.

5. **[v0.8-second-look.png]**
   New: the second look. Missed something? The report reopens the file with your pins
   where they were and every miss marked in amber. Click a mark and Lucien tells you why.

6. **[v0.8-notebook-red-flags.png]**
   The Detective's Notebook: 17 red flags and 14 yellow herrings (things that look bad and
   are fine). It fills in as you play. Every page has a drill.

## Feature tweets

- **[v0.8-title-the-desk.png]** Everything on the desk does something. Sip the coffee, pet
  the cat, click the lamp, turn the radio dial, open the safe. The window has weather. The
  phone runs a browser. Lucien has opinions.

- **[v0.8-clip-red-flag-rush.gif]** Red Flag Rush: sixty seconds, one evidence page at a
  time, click the red flag. Herrings cost you five seconds. Blank paper costs two. Streaks
  multiply. Own leaderboard.

- **[v0.8-clip-herring-hunt.gif]** The mirror of a drill: five printed pages that each carry
  something scary-looking and harmless. Click the thing that only looks bad. Learn to
  leave the innocent alone.

- **[v0.8-cold-case-printed-file.png]** Cold cases: the printer makes up a file on the spot,
  from the same red flags and herrings as the campaign. Endless. A seed in the share link
  brings the exact same file back for a friend.

- **[v0.8-clip-office-colours.gif]** Five office colours: Noir, Old file, Blue hour,
  Newsprint, Speakeasy. The whole desk is drawn from twelve named colours, so a theme is
  twelve new values and a repaint.

- **[v0.8-clip-weather.gif]** Click the window. Rain, storm, snow, fog, a clear night with a
  moon. Wish on a falling star (there's a badge).

- **[v0.8-handbook-how-to-play.png]** Too many features? The desk opens up a piece at a
  time now, and the notebook has a handbook: eighteen short pages on how the office works.
  "How to play" on the title opens it.

- **[v0.8-hall-of-detectives.png]** The Hall of Detectives: your runs under a name, a
  14-day streak strip, the rush and cold-case boards. Local by default; a shared board is
  one env var away.

- **[v0.8-rogues-gallery.png]** Every rug you call correctly puts a face on the wall.
  Procedural mugshots, charges, a reward. The Tailor is still at large.

- **[v0.8-coin-page-phantom-connected.png]** Phantom connects read-only: an address, two
  balances, a tier. It never asks you to sign anything. The game plays the same without it.

- **[v0.8-the-wall-making-of.png]** The corkboard behind the desk is the making-of: every
  notable build, pinned as a polaroid with red string. Click one to look closer.

- **[v0.8-clip-desk-toys.gif]** Coffee, lamp, cat, radio, a typed word. Try typing "moon"
  on the title. Or "biscuit". Or the other one.
